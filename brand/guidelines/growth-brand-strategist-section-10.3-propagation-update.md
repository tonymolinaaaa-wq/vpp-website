**Framework name:** Trustworthy Desert Craftsman Identity (locked v1.0, May 2026)

**Creator / origin:** Brand identity locked from approved VPP logos by the Creative
Production Advisor, May 2026. See full guidelines in companion document
`vpp-brand-guidelines-v1.0.md`. This section is the strategic summary; the Creative
Production Advisor is the source of truth for all visual specifications.

**Brand idea:** Valley Painting Pros should look like a clean, licensed Arizona
contractor that homeowners can trust inside their kitchen — not like a discount painter,
franchise chain, generic handyman, luxury remodeler, or tech-adjacent service.

**Positioning sentence:**

> Valley Painting Pros is the East Valley Arizona painting contractor for homeowners
> who want clean prep, durable finishes, and a contractor they can actually verify —
> AZ ROC #363664, bonded, and insured, with cabinet refinishing as the only current service.

**Visual identity principles:**

| Principle | Rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed trust first | AZ ROC #363664, insured, bonded, review rating, and real project photos should appear more often than decorative graphics. | Local homeowners worry about contractor reliability more than clever branding. |
| Warm desert craft, not corporate | Use terracotta (#C24A22), warm charcoal (#1F1A14), cream (#F2EAD7), and sage (#7A9577). Avoid cool blues, teal, navy, or cool grays. Avoid distressed wood textures and cartoon paint splashes. | Signals Arizona locality without looking cheap, dated, or generic-corporate. |
| Slab-serif weight, not geometric minimalism | Display typography is heavy slab serif. Body is humanist sans. The combination reads craft-forward and substantial. | The logo's slab-serif construction must be reinforced, not contradicted, in surrounding typography. |
| Cabinet refinishing proof | Lead with smooth cabinet finishes, prep shots, and before/after transformations. | Cabinet refinishing is the wedge service and has stronger visual contrast than many wall-painting jobs. |
| Human accountability | Use owner/crew photos, uniform shots, and branded vehicle shots — all real, never AI-fabricated. | Reduces contractor-anxiety and supports the local/family/craftsman archetype. |

**Locked VPP color palette (v1.0):**

| Role | Color name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Charcoal | `#1F1A14` | Wordmark, body text on light backgrounds, dark mode page background |
| Primary brand | Terracotta | `#C24A22` | All primary CTAs, the "P" of the monogram, accent highlights |
| Background | Cream | `#F2EAD7` | Primary page background — replaces white throughout the system |
| Surface elevated | Cream-50 | `#FAF6EC` | Cards lifted off the page, modals |
| Accent secondary | Sage | `#7A9577` | Used sparingly for tertiary accent and "success/complete" semantic states |
| Neutral border | Warm-gray-400 | `#A39B8C` | Dividers, borders, secondary UI |
| Text muted | Warm-gray-700 | `#5C544A` | Captions, helper text, secondary body |
| Action hover | Terracotta-600 | `#A83E1C` | CTA button hover state |

**Accessibility rule:** Use Terracotta `#C24A22` only on light backgrounds (validates at
~4.8:1 on Cream — AA pass for normal text) or as a filled CTA with Charcoal or Cream
text after WebAIM verification. Never use Terracotta as body text on Charcoal — fails
contrast minimum. Use Sage only for large text or graphic elements, not body.

**Locked VPP font pairing (v1.0):**

| Use case | Font | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display headlines (H1, H2, hero) | Alfa Slab One | Google Fonts (free, OFL) | Matches the slab-serif character of the VPP logo wordmark; heavy weight reinforces craft/substance |
| Sub-headings (H3, H4) | Inter 700 (Bold) | Google Fonts (free, OFL) | Hierarchy break from slab to bold sans; prevents Alfa Slab One from becoming "shouty" at sub-heading sizes |
| Body text (web, email, all UI) | Inter 400 (Regular) | Google Fonts (free, OFL) | Humanist sans designed for screen; pairs cleanly with slab-serif display |
| Estimate PDFs / proposals | Handled by Paint Scout | — | Paint Scout's branding configuration applies the locked palette and the closest available font; estimate template not built independently |
| Yard signs / truck lettering | Alfa Slab One in all caps | Google Fonts (free, OFL) | Heavy slab serif reads from street distance; reinforces brand consistency across applications |

**Critical typography rule:** Never use Alfa Slab One below H3 size — it becomes
unreadable as body type. Never stack Alfa Slab One in three-line headlines — visual
weight collapses; cap at two lines.

**Logo usage rules (summary — full rules in `vpp-brand-guidelines-v1.0.md`):**

| Rule | Specification |
|---|---|
| Clear space | Minimum equals the height of the "V" in the monogram on all sides |
| Minimum size | Stacked logo: 140px digital / 1.25 in print. Compact horizontal: 200px / 1.5 in. Monogram: 32px / 0.5 in |
| Background | Full-color logo on Cream or white. One-color reversed on Charcoal or dark photo overlays. One-color dark for single-color print applications. |
| Do not | Stretch, recolor outside palette, add drop shadows, rotate, place over busy photo without solid plate, or merge with trust signals (ROC, insured, bonded stay as a separate trust bar near the logo, not inside it) |
| Lockup | AZ ROC #363664, insured, bonded, and review badges form a trust bar near the logo or CTA — never inside the logo mark itself |

**Applied layout rules (East Valley positioning is default):**

| Asset | VPP identity application |
|---|---|
| Website hero | Cream background, Charcoal headline in Alfa Slab One, Terracotta CTA button, real cabinet before/after image, trust bar reading "AZ ROC #363664 · Bonded · $1M/$2M Insured" below CTA |
| Meta static ad | Large real before/after photo, small Charcoal-on-Cream trust badge "AZ ROC #363664 · Bonded · Insured," short Alfa Slab One headline on a Cream panel, CTA button in Terracotta |
| Door hanger | Top: "Cabinet Refinishing in Your Neighborhood." Middle: real before/after pair. Bottom: QR code, phone, AZ ROC #363664 trust bar. Use Sage only as a thin underline or accent element, never as a primary surface. |
| Estimate packet | Paint Scout-generated. Apply locked palette and font choices in Paint Scout's branding settings. Trust signals (AZ ROC #363664, insurance, bond, warranty) appear in proof sidebar. |
| Truck door / vehicle | Charcoal door → reversed (cream/terracotta) logo. Compact horizontal lockup for the side of the truck; monogram alone for back window or rear panel. |

**Worked example: cabinet refinishing Meta ad look (locked v1.0)**

Use a split before/after image of oak cabinets becoming warm cream-painted cabinets
(real VPP job, never AI-fabricated). Place a small top-left Charcoal-on-Cream badge:
"AZ ROC #363664 · Bonded · Insured." Put the headline in a Cream panel:
"Refresh Your Kitchen Without Replacing Your Cabinets" — set in Alfa Slab One,
Charcoal. CTA button: "Get a Free Cabinet Estimate" — set in Inter 500 on a Terracotta
fill. Use Sage only as a thin 2px underline beneath the trust badge if at all. Never
introduce additional colors.

**When to use this vs. alternatives:**

| Use VPP identity system when... | Use general visual design principles when... | Use campaign-specific art direction when... |
|---|---|---|
| Creating anything public-facing for Valley Painting Pros. | Auditing layout, hierarchy, accessibility, or photo quality independent of brand. | A temporary campaign needs a seasonal look — e.g., monsoon exterior prep, holiday cabinet refresh, summer interior cooling tones (must stay within the locked palette). |

**Advisor rule:** If a new asset does not look licensed, local, clean, and cabinet-
capable within three seconds — and does not use the locked terracotta-charcoal-cream
palette and slab-serif display typography — revise it before publishing.

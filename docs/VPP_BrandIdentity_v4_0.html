01 Brand Foundation
Strategic Position
VPP is the licensed, credentialed cabinet refinishing specialist for East Valley homeowners who want the job done right the first time — by a contractor they can verify before the first call is made.
Three Brand Pillars
Credentialed
The ROC license (#363664, CR-34 Dual) is VPP's primary trust asset for zero-awareness market entry. No unlicensed competitor can claim it. Every asset leads with it. This is not a compliance obligation — it is the most powerful differentiator VPP currently owns.
Local
East Valley — Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Queen Creek — is VPP's territory. Not generic Phoenix Metro. Not "Arizona." The geography is specific because trust is neighborhood-level. A homeowner trusts a contractor who works their street more than one who works the whole metro.
Exacting
VPP closes on quality, not price. The target homeowner chooses VPP because the standard of work removes doubt — not because the quote was the lowest. Every brand touchpoint reinforces precision: specific timelines, specific prices, specific scope. No hedging.
What VPP Is Not
A luxury lifestyle brand
A national franchise or chain
A price-driven generalist painter
A corporate vendor — this is a person's company
Growth Phase Context
This identity is built for Phase 1 — Zero-Awareness Market Entry. VPP is transitioning from 100% GC subcontract revenue (Freedom Painting, now ended) to direct-to-homeowner pipeline. The brand's job at this stage is to earn trust from a cold prospect who has no reason to believe VPP yet. Warmth, community narrative, and portfolio-led strategy are Phase 2 priorities — after direct revenue is established and a before/after portfolio is built. This identity was designed for what VPP has now, not what it aspires to have.

Pairing Rule
DM Serif Display and Outfit never compete. DM Serif Display owns the brand name, all asset headlines, and primary statements only. Outfit owns everything else — credentials, body copy, labels, CTAs, contact information, prices, and metadata. If a line of copy is functional, it is Outfit. If it carries the brand name or a primary emotional statement, it is DM Serif Display. No exceptions, no mixing on a single line.

06 Brand Voice
Five Voice Principles
01
Credential first
Lead every asset with what VPP can prove. The ROC license, the dual certification, the specific number of years in market. Not a claim — a fact the homeowner can verify before the first call.
02
Specific beats general
"$2,250 for a 20-door kitchen completed in 3 days" closes. "Affordable, quality cabinet refinishing" does not. Every price, timeline, and scope statement is specific. No ranges without anchors. No promises without numbers.
03
No hedging
"We try," "we hope," "we think," "most of the time" — none of these appear. VPP makes statements, not qualifications. If the statement cannot be made without a hedge, it should not be made at all.
04
One sentence, one job
Every line of copy does exactly one thing: builds a credential, states an offer, establishes a timeline, or calls to action. The moment a sentence tries to do two things, it does neither well. Stacking claims creates noise.
05
East Valley geography
The location is always present and always specific. "East Valley" or named cities — Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Queen Creek — not "Phoenix Metro," not "Arizona," not "the Valley." Specificity is a trust signal in local service markets.
06
Warm but direct
The tone is confident and human — not corporate, not cold, not scripted. Ricardo's company, Ricardo's voice. The warmth comes from clarity and honesty, not from adjectives. "We'll be done Thursday" is warmer than "we value your time."
Voice by Mode — Four Contexts
Mode 1 — Awareness (Cold Prospect)
Rule
Lead with credential. State the service and geography. Give them one specific number (price anchor or timeline). One CTA. Nothing else.
"Cabinet refinishing from $2,250. Licensed contractor. East Valley. AZ ROC #363664. Free quote — call or text."
Never
"Transform your kitchen today!"
"We're the best painters in the valley!"
"Quality work at affordable prices"
Mode 2 — Consideration (Evaluating Options)
Rule
Address the comparison directly. State what VPP does that unlicensed competitors cannot. Specific proof: ROC number, number of completed jobs, timeline, written quote guarantee.
"Not all painters are licensed. VPP is — ROC #363664, CR-34 Dual. Written quote before any work starts. If we miss the timeline, we make it right."
Never
"We're different from other painters"
"Customer satisfaction guaranteed!"
Mode 3 — Conversion (Ready to Book)
Rule
Remove friction. Confirm the scope, confirm the price, confirm the date. One clear next step. No upselling at this stage. Make it easy and obvious to say yes.
"Here's your quote: 22 cabinet doors, $2,750, done by Friday. Sign here and we'll confirm the start date today."
Never
"While we're there, we could also…"
Multiple CTAs — pick one
Mode 4 — Retention (Existing Client)
Rule
Personal, brief, direct. This is a person talking to someone they've worked with, not a marketing message. Reference the specific job. Ask for the review directly. Make the referral ask feel natural.
"The kitchen looks great — appreciate you trusting us with it. If you have 60 seconds, a Google review helps us more than anything. Here's the direct link."
Never
Mass email blast language
"Dear Valued Customer"
07 Photography Standards
VPP's direct-to-homeowner portfolio is currently being built as of March 2026. All previous work was sold under Freedom Painting's brand identity — VPP has no existing before/after library for its own direct pipeline. Every direct job from this point forward requires a before/after pair. The portfolio protocol below must be followed on every job. Within 6–8 direct jobs, VPP will have enough real photography to activate the portfolio strategy.
Always Do
✓Shoot before/after pairs from the identical angle and camera position — the transformation must be directly comparable side by side
✓Shoot in natural light. Open windows, turn off overhead fluorescents if they cast yellow or green
✓Clear the counter and remove clutter before the "after" shot — the photo sells the result, not just the cabinets
✓Capture the full cabinet run (wide shot) AND a close-up detail (door face, edge, hardware) — both are required for every job
✓Get the client's written permission to use photos in marketing before the job ends — use the standard permission line in the contract
✓File naming: [YYMMDD]_[ClientLastName]_[Before/After]_[01].jpg
✓Shoot the same job from multiple angles — front-facing wide, detail close-up, corner perspective. Options matter at asset build time.
Never Do
✕Use stock photography or AI-generated kitchen images — only real VPP completed jobs, always
✕Use a before/after pair where the angles differ — it looks like two different kitchens and destroys credibility
✕Use blurry, dark, cluttered, or poorly composed photos on any asset — a bad photo is worse than no photo
✕Apply VPP brand overlays or watermarks that obscure the work itself
✕Use the same photo on both panels of a two-sided door hanger
✕Deploy photos from any job where the client has not given permission
✕Use photos from Freedom Painting-era jobs as VPP portfolio images — that work belongs to another brand

10 ROC Compliance — Standing Requirements
These compliance items are permanent requirements under Arizona law and ROC regulations. They apply to every customer-facing asset regardless of format, channel, or production method. The Governance Advisor must clear all items before any asset deploys.
ROC #363664 Displayed Legibly
Required on all printed advertising. The number must be legible at the asset's intended viewing distance — not buried in footer text at 6pt. Acceptable formats: "AZ ROC #363664" or "Arizona ROC License #363664."
A.R.S. § 32-1124(B) · Required, no exception
Company Name Present
"Valley Painting Pros" must appear on every asset. The name must match the ROC registration exactly.
A.R.S. § 32-1124(B)
Services Match CR-34 Scope
All advertised services must fall within CR-34 Painting & Wallcovering scope. VPP may not advertise general contracting, drywall installation, flooring, or any trade outside the license. Drywall repair as a painting-prep service is within scope.
CR-34 Painting & Wallcovering — Dual Residential + Commercial
"Licensed · Bonded · Insured" Requires Active Coverage
This phrase is a legal claim. Before any asset carrying this language deploys: confirm contractor bond is current at required amount AND confirm GL insurance is active at required coverage. If either lapses, this phrase must be removed from distribution immediately.
ROC Compliance Master v1.0 — Section 03
URL Exception Rule — Digital Assets
H.B. 2545 (Sept 29, 2021): website URL may substitute the ROC# only if AZ ROC #363664 appears on the valleypaintingpros.com homepage — not footer only. If the homepage does not display the number, all digital assets must carry it directly.
H.B. 2545 · Effective Sept 29, 2021
Warranty Claims Require Written Instrument
Any warranty language ("5-year warranty," "guaranteed for life") requires a written warranty document defining scope, exclusions, and remedies before it may appear on any asset. Without the document, warranty claims create breach-of-implied-warranty exposure under Arizona consumer protection law.
Arizona Consumer Protection · ROC Complaint Risk

## Brand And Service Scope

Valley Painting Pros is an East Valley Arizona cabinet refinishing specialist.

Customer-facing ads should promote cabinet refinishing only. Do not present VPP as a general painting business or advertise non-cabinet services.

Brand voice:

- Confident, plainspoken, local, and trade-specific
- Warm but not salesy
- Short sentences
- "We" language is okay
- Avoid hype, fake urgency, fake scarcity, invented testimonials, and broad contractor fluff

Primary trust signal:

- AZ ROC #363664

Approved service area wording:

- East Valley
- Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa, Tempe, Scottsdale, Queen Creek

## Core Offer

VPP cabinet refinishing is priced at:

**$150 per opening**

An opening means one cabinet door or one drawer face. Doors and drawers are priced identically.

The price is fixed in writing before work begins.

Typical kitchen examples:

| Kitchen Size | Doors | Drawers | Total Openings | Price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | 14 | 8 | 22 | $3,300 |
| Medium | 20 | 12 | 32 | $4,800 |
| Large | 28 | 16 | 44 | $6,600 |

Price anchor:

- Cabinet replacement commonly runs $8,000-$25,000+
- VPP refinishing is typically $3,300-$6,600 for most East Valley kitchens
- Key approved line: "A $15,000 kitchen remodel result for $4,800."

Do not create pricing tiers, separate add-ons, different drawer-face prices, or upsell language.

## Included In Every Cabinet Refinishing Job

Use these as the complete offer stack. Ricardo may select only some of these for a specific ad.

### 1. Professional Cabinet Refinishing

Public-facing description:

- Professional cabinet refinishing using a professional-grade waterborne urethane finish
- Dedicated cabinet coatings, not regular wall paint repurposed for a kitchen
- Commercial-grade durability for daily kitchen use
- Vertical spray process for a smooth, consistent finish
- Do not use the 76-step process as customer-facing ad copy; it is internal SOP language.

Customer value:

- The kitchen gets a high-end, finished look without replacing the cabinets.
- The finish is built for everyday kitchen use, not a quick cosmetic coat.

Ad-friendly phrases:

- Professional cabinet refinishing
- Commercial-grade durability for daily kitchen use
- Dedicated cabinet coatings
- Smooth sprayed finish

### 2. Soft-Close Hinge Upgrade

Public-facing description:

- New soft-close hinges are included on every eligible cabinet door.
- VPP sources, installs, and adjusts the hinges.
- The upgrade is included in the offer, not sold as a separate line item.

Customer value:

- No more slamming cabinet doors.
- The kitchen feels newer and quieter immediately.
- The customer does not have to source hinges or figure out fitment.

Ad-friendly phrases:

- Soft-close hinges included
- New soft-close hinges on eligible doors
- We source, install, and adjust them
- Quieter cabinets from day one

### 3. Hardware Audit

Public-facing description:

- VPP checks handle and knob alignment.
- Old or misaligned holes are patched.
- Hardware holes are redrilled straight where needed.
- This applies whether the customer keeps existing hardware or installs new hardware.

Customer value:

- Handles line up correctly.
- Crooked builder-grade hardware issues get corrected instead of painted around.
- The finished cabinets look intentional, not just repainted.

Ad-friendly phrases:

- Hardware audit included
- Old holes patched
- Handles redrilled straight
- Cleaner alignment on the finished cabinets

### 4. Felt Pads

Public-facing description:

- Felt pads are installed on cabinet doors and drawers after refinishing.

Customer value:

- Doors and drawers close more quietly.
- Pads help protect the fresh finish from finish-to-finish contact.

Ad-friendly phrases:

- Felt pads included
- Quieter closes
- Finish protection from day one

### 5. Cabinet Label Preservation

Public-facing description:

- VPP identifies and preserves important cabinet labels when present.
- Original labels can include builder labels, certification labels, emergency contact stickers, or utility information.

Customer value:

## Guardrails For ChatGPT

When creating ads from this source:

- Use only cabinet refinishing language.
- Use "$150 per opening" as the pricing unit.
- Treat doors and drawers as identical openings for pricing.
- Use "5-year warranty in writing" for warranty language.
- Use "AZ ROC #363664" for license credibility.
- Do not name specific paint product brands.
- Do not use the 76-step process as public ad copy.
- Do not use "lifetime touch-up kit" language.
- Do not invent testimonials, reviews, awards, certifications, or scarcity.
- Do not imply every kitchen is identical; use "most kitchens" for turnaround language.
- Do not cram all inclusions into one ad unless Ricardo specifically asks for a full value-stack asset.
- If Ricardo selects specific inclusions, make those inclusions the focus and leave the others out or reduce them to small supporting copy.

01 The Mark — Anatomy
The VPP mark is a typographic system, not an icon-based identity. The primary visual element is the wordmark — "Valley Painting Pros" set in DM Serif Display with the italic terracotta "Pros" as the signature element. The 2px terracotta rule line below the wordmark is structural — it separates the brand name from the credential tagline and creates the visual anchor that makes the lockup cohesive at every scale. The mark contains no custom icon, illustration, or decorative symbol. The credential tagline ("Licensed · East Valley · AZ ROC #363664") completes the lockup and is not optional on the full variant.

03 Clearspace Rules
Clearspace is the minimum empty space maintained around the logo on every application. Nothing — text, images, graphics, other logos, or decorative elements — may enter this zone. The clearspace unit (X) is defined as the cap-height of the "V" in the wordmark at whatever size the logo is set. This makes the rule proportional and self-scaling across every application.

Clearspace Unit — X
X = cap-height of the "V" in the wordmark at the applied size. At 36px wordmark, X ≈ 20px. At 72px, X ≈ 40px. The rule is always 1X on all four sides.
What Violates Clearspace
Body copy, headlines, image edges, decorative borders, other logos, QR codes, phone numbers, or any graphic element entering the 1X zone on any side.
Door Hanger Exception
On very constrained assets (cabinet label, business card back), clearspace may be reduced to 0.75X if — and only if — nothing enters the zone. Reducing clearspace to accommodate other elements is never acceptable.
Mark Only — Asset 03
Same rule applies to the standalone monogram. 1X clearspace on all sides. The border of the VP box is not the edge of the mark — the rule line below it is. Measure from the rule line.

05 Misuse — Never Do This
Do not recolor "Pros"
Valley Painting Pros
"Pros" is always terracotta #C4613A. Never signal blue, sage, black, gold, or any other color. The italic terracotta treatment is the brand's signature — changing it breaks the system.
Do not remove the italic
Valley Painting Pros
"Pros" is always italic DM Serif Display. Setting it in roman weight removes the warmth signal that differentiates the wordmark. The italic is structural, not stylistic.
Do not use on dark backgrounds
Valley Painting Pros
Asset 01 is for light surfaces only. On dark backgrounds, the ink wordmark disappears. Use Asset 04B (compact dark) instead — it is built for dark surfaces.
Do not stretch or distort
Valley Pros
Never stretch, condense, skew, rotate, or apply transformations to the wordmark. If the space requires a different proportion, use a different variant from the step-down ladder.
Do not remove the rule line
Valley Painting Pros
Licensed · East Valley · AZ ROC #363664
The 2px terracotta rule line is not optional. It separates the wordmark from the credential line and is a structural element of the lockup. Without it, the lockup loses its visual anchor.
Do not use cabinet variant globally
Cabinet Refinishing
Valley Painting Pros
East Valley's Cabinet Specialists
Assets 05 and 06 (cabinet variants) are for cabinet-specific marketing only. Placing them on a truck wrap, uniform, or business card narrows VPP's positioning to a single service line. Use Assets 01–04 for all full-company representation.
06 Favicon & Digital Icon Spec
The favicon uses Asset 03 (mark only) — the VP monogram in its minimal form. At 16×16 and 32×32 pixels, the full serif letterforms cannot render cleanly. The favicon is a simplified version: the "VP" letterforms reduced to their essential shapes, the terracotta rule line maintained as a 1px stroke at 16px and 2px at 32px+.
